## AI-Based Early Cancer Detection Using Medical Imaging Platform
An AI-powered healthcare platform that assists in the early detection of cancer using medical imaging and machine learning technologies.
## Problem Statement

Cancer remains one of the leading causes of death worldwide. Early detection significantly increases the chances of successful treatment and survival. However, traditional diagnostic methods can be time-consuming, expensive, and dependent on specialist availability.

In many healthcare systems, delayed diagnosis due to manual analysis of medical images leads to late-stage cancer detection and reduced treatment effectiveness.

This project aims to solve these challenges by developing an AI-powered medical imaging platform capable of assisting healthcare professionals in identifying cancer at earlier stages with improved accuracy and efficiency.

# 🏗️ System Architecture

The platform follows a client-server architecture integrated with AI prediction services.

## Workflow

1. User uploads medical imaging data.
2. Frontend sends the image to the backend server.
3. Backend processes the image and forwards it to the AI model.
4. AI model analyzes the image and predicts cancer probability.
5. Results are stored in the database.
6. Prediction reports are displayed on the dashboard.
